National Geographic-11.2026

This National Geographic special issue (Nov 2026), produced with Rolex’s Perpetual Planet Ocean Expeditions, explores Earth’s single interconnected yet threatened ocean. Covering global sites, it features research on resilient corals in Cook Islands, pygmy blue whales and krill off New Zealand, Seychelles’ deep‑sea exploration, Gambian mangroves, Antarctic methane seeps, Pacific traditional navigation and Raja Ampat’s conservation challenges. Scientists examine climate‑driven disruptions, local community cooperation and hopeful restoration solutions, urging public commitment to safeguard our fragile marine biome.
